## Spiked Eggnog 

1. Rim the glass with eggnog, then rim the glass with a cinnamon sugar mixture. Set glass aside.  
2. Fill cocktail shaker with the following ingredients and shake:
    - 1 vanilla vodka
    - 1 Disaronno liqueur
    - 4 eggnog 
    - 1/4 tsp of nutmeg or freshly grated nutmeg  
3. Pour in the glass with cinnamon sugar rim 

## Cinnamon & Sugar Mixture

The most common ratio is about 3-4 parts sugar to 1 part cinnamon, though you can adjust to taste. For example:

- 3 tablespoons sugar + 1 tablespoon cinnamon, or
- 1/4 cup sugar + 1 tablespoon cinnamon
